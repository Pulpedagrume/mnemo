## Personal data

- **No collection.** This site has no account, no form, no analytics, no advertising, and it does
  not call any third-party service (except the optional AI model download, below).
- **Your data stays on your device.** Decks, cards, review history and settings are stored only in
  your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). They leave your device only if you export a file, or
  if you enable sync with a server of your choice yourself: whoever runs that server is then
  responsible for that data.
- **No cookies, no trackers.** Local storage is only used to run the app (collection, theme,
  language). It is strictly necessary for the service you request, so no consent is needed.
- **Host logs.** As with any website, the host may record technical connection data (including
  the IP address) to keep the service secure, under its own privacy policy (link above).
- **AI assistant (optional, off by default).** If you enable it, the AI model (Qwen3.5, Alibaba
  Cloud, Apache-2.0 license) is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and its runtime
  from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com): these services then see your IP address, under their
  own privacy policies. The model then runs entirely in your browser: your questions and cards
  are never sent. Answers are generated by an AI and may contain mistakes. You can remove the
  model at any time (Settings → AI assistant).
- **Deletion.** Export a backup first if you wish (Settings → Backup), then clear the site data in
  your browser settings or uninstall the app.
- **Complaints.** You can contact the publisher or your data protection authority (in France, the
  CNIL: https://www.cnil.fr).
